Effective 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Elite Chess (“we”) handles personal information in the Elite Chess app, the website elitechess.online and the offline copy (the “Service”). We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and we respect the rights of users in other places, including the EU and UK.

The short version

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and we never sell your information.
  • Your profile, settings, progress and games are stored on your own device.
  • Online play connects you directly to the other player’s device, which means they can see your internet (IP) address.

1. Information on your device

Your display name, avatar, country (if you choose one), settings, progress, puzzles, achievements, saved games and friends list are stored in your browser or app storage on your device. We do not receive them unless you sign in to an account (section 3). You can delete them with Settings → Reset all progress, or by clearing the app’s or browser’s data.

2. Online play, friends and tournaments

When you play online, add friends or join a tournament, the Service uses the PeerJS connection service to introduce your device to the other player’s device, and then connects the two devices directly (WebRTC). To do this, your device shares with the other players and with the connection service: your display name, avatar, country flag, rating, friend code, your moves and game details, and your device’s internet (IP) address. The other player’s device can therefore see your IP address, which can show your approximate location. If you do not want this, do not use the online features.

3. Accounts (when available)

If you create an account, we use Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) to sign you in and to keep your progress in step across devices. Firebase stores your email address, an account ID, your sign-in details and a copy of your progress. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. Firebase servers may be outside Australia, including in the United States.

4. Other services the Service connects to

  • Hosting and updates: the website and app updates are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it receives your IP address and basic browser details when it serves files.
  • Live sports scores: the Sport features request scores from ESPN’s public scoreboards and from our site. Those providers receive your IP address and basic browser details.
  • Chess engine and fonts: these run on your device. They send nothing anywhere.

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies. Some of them are located outside Australia, mainly in the United States.

5. Why we use information

Only to run the features you choose: to let you play, to connect you with other players, to keep your progress and to keep the Service secure and fair. For users in the EU and UK, our lawful bases are performing our agreement with you (the Terms) and our legitimate interest in running a secure, fair service.

6. Children

Kids mode and play against the computer work without sending personal information anywhere. Online features and accounts are for people aged 13 or over, and under-18s need a parent or guardian’s permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security and keeping information

Connections use encryption (HTTPS and encrypted WebRTC). Information on your device stays there until you delete it. Account information is kept until you ask us to delete your account.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and (in the EU and UK) to restrict or object to its use or to receive a copy. If you are not happy with how we handle a request, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au), or to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.
